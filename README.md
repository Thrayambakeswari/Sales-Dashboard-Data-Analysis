# Sales-Dashboard-Data-Analysis
This project transforms static internet sales reports into interactive Power BI dashboards for tracking product and customer sales over time. It includes filters for sales reps, compares actuals to the 2021 budget, refreshes daily, and uses SQL and Excel data to support better decision-making and sales performance.

<a href = "https://github.com/Thrayambakeswari/Sales-Dashboard-Data-Analysis/blob/main/FACT_InternetSales.csv"> InternalSales </a>
## KPI'S 
1. Total Internet Sales – Tracks overall revenue from online sales.
2. Sales vs. Budget (%) – Measures actual sales against 2021 budget targets.
3. Top-Selling Products – Identifies the highest-performing products.
4. Sales by Customer – Highlights key clients contributing to sales.
5. Sales Growth Over Time – Shows trends in monthly/quarterly sales.
6. Sales by Salesperson – Measures individual performance across products and customers.
